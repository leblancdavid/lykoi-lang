# R6.48 decision supplement

1. Use the authorized same-coordinator fallback transparently when no independent
   commissioner/reviewer/custodian is supplied. Passing finite behavior cannot
   override the independent admission gate.
2. Freeze family assignments and all complete source-side contracts/expectations
   before construction. Preserve ambiguous sources as pending, without inventing
   expiry, fairness or authority decisions.
3. Pair exact direct artifacts with actual scripted API traces, explicitly keeping
   unavailable AI semantic trajectories null. Do not turn scripted calls or
   expansions into additional requirement examples.
4. Preserve whole-contract distributed capability and acceptance gaps. Do not
   substitute bounded one-store state or actor selectors for remote atomicity,
   physical effects or authentication. Do not change primitives to fit tasks.
5. Publish an empty training-ready export rather than promoting finite-accepted
   exposed examples. Classify the process partial and the split a demonstration.
   Recommend one independently clarified local-stateful family before scaling.

round-local decisions; historical classifications and shared guidance are preserved.
