# R6.31 design decisions

1. **Separate H1/H2.** Discovery uses the qualified composition subset; stateful
   modification uses production profiles. Combining them would confound vocabulary
   selection with edit machinery and exceed the experimental wrapper's semantics.
2. **Six H1 conditions.** Primitive-only success cannot distinguish adaptive selection
   from ordinary macros. Human actual-capacity matching and expanded-template ablation
   are required attribution controls; failed matching limits claims rather than
   licensing a weaker comparator.
3. **H2 accepted bases and real caller edits.** Score changed existing consumers and
   retained behavior separately with a frozen supersession map. Historical originals
   remain immutable, but deployed successor behavior must actually change.
4. **Full workflow cost.** Include proposal failures, retrieval, lowering, library
   maintenance and new setup. Missing usage is null; shorter representations or SDK
   cost0 cannot establish lower total cost.
5. **Bounded implementation first.** Small experiment-local facades and filesystem
   manifests suffice prospectively; no semantic extensions, large registry project
   or training. Qualify with scripted exposed fixtures before author trials.
6. **Evidence grades are explicit.** Enforced withholding and procedural separation
   are distinguished; unattested isolation yields exploratory evidence. Practical
   thresholds do not become significance claims from small studies.

These are prospective choices in the [design](../benchmark/results/phase6/R6_31-REPORT.md),
not adoption of a production architecture. Stop pending separate authorization.
