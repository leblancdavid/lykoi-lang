# Lightweight generalization protocol 2 — R5.118A

Prospective amendment to [protocol 1](phase6-generalization-protocol-r5.115.md),
using [research approval 1](research-evaluation-approval-v1.md). The earlier
protocol and R5.116/R5.116A/R5.117/R5.118 records retain historical force; this
amendment neither changes first results nor authorizes a requirement evaluation.

## Approval choice before authoring

Declare whether the attempt is a product-approved experiment or a **research-only**
experiment. Record source behavioral authority, research authorization and product
approval in separate fields, with `NOT_ESTABLISHED` where absent. An external request
need not be approved by its maintainers to support a legitimate research experiment.
Research approval certifies permission to test a declared interpretation, not that
interpretation's fidelity to definitive upstream intent or production suitability.

For research-only attempts, protocol 1 step 3's upstream/owner approval prerequisite
is replaced prospectively by an explicitly appointed research approver's exact
`RESEARCH_EVALUATION_APPROVED` decision. Material source questions remain blocking.
Do not guess omitted behavior, default activation, migration, roles or errors. A
bounded source delta need not respecify unrelated application behavior.

## Procedure and retained evidence

1. **Separate authorization and snapshot:** obtain authorization for this one source
   access/attempt; record fresh commit/tree/diff, relevant core/V1/profile/model/test
   identities and kernel 26 before access, retaining exposure disclosures. No machine,
   model, OpenCode, isolation-service or runtime-eligibility gates are added.
2. **Bind source and candidate:** retain exact external origin/revision/capture bytes
   and hash; source attribution is behavioral evidence, not upstream product approval.
   Extract source-only inventory and reconcile it with the exact FRC and declared scope.
3. **Review and fix acceptance:** one initial review and at most two correction passes
   per exact FRC. Prefer another context; disclose same-agent/same-model limitations.
   Fix source-derived obligations, positive/negative/boundary/preservation checks,
   exact expected observations and coverage limitations **before authoring**. Record
   exact `research_plan` identity, native payload where used, and review identity.
   Independent here means derived without generated implementation as the source of
   expectations. Cognitive independence is separately disclosed, never inferred from
   a second pass; same-agent synthetic review is development evidence.
4. **Explicit approval:** an appointed `research_approver` or legitimate human decision
   approves exact source/FRC/assumptions/nonblocking uncertainties/plan/evaluator for
   `research-evaluation-only`. The producer may recommend, never self-authorize. If no
   authorized approver exists, halt `NEEDS_CLARIFICATION`, reason
   `RESEARCH_APPROVER_UNAVAILABLE`. Missing cognition separation alone is not an
   infrastructure halt. Unresolved material decisions still halt clarification.
5. **Consume and attempt:** designated evaluator consumes exact approval, producing
   the research evaluation record/seal. Use the existing unchanged-capability sequence
   structural coverage → BDI → adequacy → faithful V1 → restricted Lykoi authoring →
   validation/lowering → external verification. Only supported stages may authorize
   research implementation. Approval cannot create stage receipts or override refusals.
6. **Verify and publish:** compare external observations with exact pre-author criteria;
   generated software cannot certify itself. Preserve native first blocker/code, real
   stage ledger, exact artifacts/observations, bounded coverage and review limitations.
   Downstream stages are `NOT_REACHED`; reached unexecuted checks `NOT_RUN` with reason.
7. **Stop:** preserve immutable first result immediately. No repair/retry, changed
   expectations, next-source access or semantic development within this attempt. Later
   answers/approval/attempts are separately authorized linked post-exposure records.

## Additional minimum record fields

- Approval mode/purpose; exact source/FRC/review/acceptance-plan identities.
- Behavioral source authority and scope; explicit assumptions and nonblocking unknowns.
- Approver identity, role provisioning/appointment reference, decision evidence and
  designated evaluator; no AI-authored assertion of being appointed.
- Review context/access, finite budget used, same-model/agent limitations and independent
  acceptance evidence strength, distinguished from external-process observation.
- Research evaluation/seal identity; any `research-implementation` grant and native
  stage receipts; `product_what_approval: NOT_ESTABLISHED` where absent and
  `production_authorized: false` for research-only authority.
- Pre-author criterion identities, source traceability, observable checks and exclusions;
  exact native plan equality for the sealed native pipeline.

Protocol 1 taxonomy, first-attempt denominator, kernel accounting and preservation
rules otherwise continue. A research-only `SUCCESS` means finite external checks of
the source-grounded declared interpretation passed; it does not certify upstream
intent, owner endorsement, production release or universal correctness.
