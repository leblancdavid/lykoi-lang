# R6.49 training-candidate and evaluation qualification policy

## Tier T: supervised training candidate

All gates are conjunctive: authoritative source for the declared task scope;
complete source-bound FRC; faithful implementation with deterministic semantic
validation; preconstruction frozen acceptance; actual AI-free deterministic replay;
stable source, target, dependency and evidence identities; no unresolved correctness
defect or contradictory observed behavior; no known evaluation-solution leakage;
and sufficient rights or owner authorization for the proposed training use.

For an owner-authorized synthetic pilot, a preserved coordinator-authored source
is the authority for that synthetic fixture. It is not human-authored, independent,
or approved for deployment. Independent commissioning/review are preferred, not
mandatory. Capability-aware design, inherited repository context and same-coordinator
source/FRC/oracle/implementation authorship must be disclosed. Finite acceptance
supports a bounded candidate, not a proof for all inputs. An unresolved contradiction
blocks admission regardless of validation or repeated successes.

`T_CANDIDATE_ADMITTED` means eligible for this research candidate pool, not trained,
licensed for arbitrary distribution, or sufficient for broad software construction.
Rights basis here is the owner's explicit data-generation/training-candidate review
authorization over locally generated synthetic artifacts. Third-party sources require
a separate rights determination. Missing trajectories do not disqualify a complete
direct pair, but must remain null. Replicas, renamed targets and replay observations
never count as new episodes. Known evaluation solutions are excluded.

## Tier E: evaluation-qualified

Additionally require independent commissioning and acceptance review, withheld
families, enforced or explicitly qualified isolation, leakage-resistant connected
family partitions, and frozen scoring before participant exposure. Evidence for
each control must exist; absence is a failure, not assumed compliance. Tier T does
not imply Tier E. Exposed synthetic development fixtures cannot become pristine
evaluation examples merely by relabeling or random splitting.

## Reproducible admission and preservation

Bind each gate to exact artifacts, rerun validation/lowering and frozen acceptance,
compare complete observations, audit semantic duplicates and dependency ancestry,
record authorization and partitions, then seal a successor ledger and publication.
If any mandatory gate fails, preserve a pending/rejected entry with its blocker.
No outcome-derived changes to frozen expectations. Negative controls are separate.

R6.47 DATASET_PARTIAL and R6.48 DATA_PIPELINE_PARTIAL, including original zero
admission, remain unchanged. This policy prospectively separates supervised
candidate quality from independent evaluation quality rather than retroactively
weakening either historical classification.
