# Lightweight generalization protocol 3 — R5.120A

Prospective local-workflow amendment to [protocol 2](phase6-generalization-protocol-r5.118a.md)
and [protocol 1](phase6-generalization-protocol-r5.115.md). Earlier protocols,
R5.117/R5.118/R5.118A/R5.119/R5.119A/R5.120 and all first results are preserved.
This amendment authorizes no external requirement evaluation.

## Explicit workflow selection

Before execution declare either **authenticated controller** (existing approval 1,
production security unchanged) or **local research** ([local interface 1](local-research-execution-v1.md)).
Local research does not require administrator-provisioned controller credentials;
it cannot establish controller authentication, product WHAT, grants or deployment.
Research permission, behavioral source authority and production authority remain
separate. No AI self-approval or fabrication of human identity.

## Local research procedure

1. Obtain separate authorization for the exact source/attempt. Record fresh commit/tree,
   implementation/test hashes, kernel count, time and actual exposure history. Preserve
   the prior first result; a later P6-A03 attempt is explicitly a **linked post-first-result
   research attempt**, never a replacement first attempt or blinded evaluation.
2. Retain exact source origin/revision/capture/text identity, FRC identity and acceptance
   identity. Conduct source-only inventory/materiality review using the existing finite
   budget (initial review plus at most two correction passes). Retain assumptions,
   unresolved material questions, source traceability, limitations and actual review
   context. Human research permission cannot fill a material behavioral gap.
3. Fix the source-derived acceptance expectations before authorship. Supply the exact
   approved executable plan for the current external verifier. If approved artifacts
   lack that payload, halt rather than regenerate or substitute it; any separately
   authorized adapter preparation must preserve expectations and bind exact approval
   to its execution representation before authoring.
4. Preserve the exact human approval statement/provenance and named local evaluator,
   explicitly for `local-research-only`. Conversation provenance is not cryptographic
   authentication. Retain that approval independently of the receipt/author. Missing
   legitimate permission remains `NEEDS_CLARIFICATION / RESEARCH_APPROVER_UNAVAILABLE`.
5. Record the minimal local receipt, implementation content snapshot and UTC timestamp.
   Verify literal approval equality, source/FRC/acceptance identities, scope, evaluator
   declaration and snapshot. Reject mismatches before execution; no production seal.
6. Execute unchanged FRC → structural coverage → BDI → adequacy → faithful V1 → acceptance
   coverage → restricted authoring → compilation → external verification. Preserve native
   first blocker/stage. Unsupported stages halt; all downstream stages are `NOT_REACHED`.
   No controller grant or authenticated semantic receipt is inferred from local evidence.
7. Publish independently classified observations against fixed pre-author expectations,
   exact bindings, stage ledger, review limitations and `production_authorized: false`.
   If Redis-compatible behavior cannot execute on the current backend/verifier, report
   its actual first limitation; a simplified ACL simulation is not full Redis success.
8. Stop after the first terminal result. No repair/retry, changed expected outcomes,
   other-source access or semantic development. Separate permission and explicit linkage
   are required for later attempts. Local receipts are not single-use production tokens.

Protocol 1's native taxonomy/denominators and protocol 2's source-only review and
independent-acceptance obligations continue. Local success means finite external checks
passed, not universal correctness, upstream endorsement or production suitability.

## Qualification and stop

R5.120A qualifies only public synthetic contracts, including positive native execution,
legitimate unsupported-stage halts, wrong-but-compilable behavioral failure and production
isolation. Same-agent/model reasoning is disclosed separately from external-process
observation. No authentication service, qualification gate, runtime freeze, protected
source machinery or second research controller is introduced.

**Stop after synthetic qualification.** P6-A03 is not executed, authored or compiled;
P6-A04/P6-A05 remain uninspected. Interface readiness does not establish P6-A03 executable
acceptance integration or Redis capability. The immutable R5.120 first result remains
`NEEDS_CLARIFICATION / RESEARCH_APPROVER_UNAVAILABLE` under its historical workflow.
