Authorization: owner request for one nonproduction, AI-free execution qualification.
Participant inference, training, optimizer/scheduler updates and model loading are zero.
Use pinned local R6.52 tokenizer offline, CPU only. Identity checks preserve R6.72 halt,
R6.71 comparison, R6.70 decoder, R6.67 adapter, R6.65 scorer, R6.63 examples and R6.66
infrastructure/configuration and inherited production/kernel26/VM identities.

Order: baseline identity check; reserve evaluation-family identities; freeze handwritten
requirements/scopes/ordered decisions/dependencies/acceptance fixtures; implement renderer;
reconstruct and execute positives through unchanged authoritative interfaces; validate
labeled negatives and adversarial controls; inspect S11 historical prefix with CPU masks;
measure complete targets with pinned tokenizer; publish and repeat integrity checks.
No expected output may be taken from candidate execution. Coordinator-only expectation
review is disclosed. Negative decisions are contrast/feedback data, never positive SFT
assistant completions. No AI reasoning traces exist.

Bound: nine semantic contrast categories, two positive requirements each, two labeled
negatives per positive, three explicit fixtures per positive; three future exploratory
evaluation families reserved without solutions. Renaming and correlated repeats are not
new behavioral families. Operation contrasts with distinct output types necessarily carry
distinct result obligations; report this possible shortcut. Shared-prefix behavior can
mask relation differences: require exact operation evidence as well as functional evidence.

Reject unknown op, missing operand, malformed typed literal, absent reference, incorrect
deps, ambiguous scope, incompatible types, extra fields and malformed serialization.
Wrong ordered comparisons must survive reconstruction unchanged and fail acceptance.
CPU decoder analysis must distinguish grammar reachability, token mask, full-prefix versus
singleton decode, budget and EOS; preserve historical failure and never rescore it.

Publication is additive. Existing compiler/lowerer/runtime/VM and all historical results
remain byte-preserved. No P6-A04 acceptance or P6-A05 access. Final qualification requires
positive reconstruction/behavior, correctly labeled negative results and passing adversarial
controls; no claim of learning/generalization. Stop after publication and await authorization.
