## Limits and required completion answers

Local role/process fixtures, empty environments and Python API denial are implemented,
**not OS/native-code containment or model/provider independence**. Service credential
provisioning, fixture registry and SQLite are trusted. Generated programs are not OS
sandboxed. Structural/plan reviewers remain synthetic bounded evidence; exact component
authority is preserved, not independently rediscovered by BDI. Textual invariant/error
descriptions alone are not executed assertions. No general AI author runs; faithful
mapping and independently executable verifier coverage remain narrow.

1. **Authoring without exact grant? No.** Applicability and single-use reservation gate dispatch.
2. **Acceptance sealed before author task visibility? Yes.** Seal precedes bundle creation.
3. **Author can alter acceptance? No through this interface.** Role, immutable identities,
   native plan validation and exact manifest prevent it; replacements invalidate old runs.
4. **Unsupported analysis/V1 visibly halt before authoring? Yes.** Faults and actual public attempt show this.
5. **Target bound to exact inputs/components? Yes.** Model/run/grant/compiler/manifest and deterministic target checks.
6. **Independent verification tests behavior? Yes.** External observations, never code-identity acceptance.
7. **Incorrect compilable implementation can fail? Yes.** Registered calibration fault and unchanged-plan default probe.
8. **Restart reconstruction? Yes for completed/halted runs.** Immutable terminal/dependency/journal evidence, fresh-process test.
9. **Rehearsal blockers?** Unqualified production semantic producers/reviewers, narrow
   faithful mappings/plan coverage, fixture-only authoring, lack of OS/hostile-code
   containment and independent-model qualification, and a separately authorized,
   prefrozen public rehearsal with its declared environment/corpus.
