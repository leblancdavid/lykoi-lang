## Immediate pre/post verification and TOCTOU

The gate stages canonical capsule, certificate and every required receipt, then
locks their bytes and the workspace marker with the R5.43 recorder. Immediately
before reservation it verifies workspace ownership, live locks, authority,
contamination, protected evidence, zero accounting and a fresh matching capsule/
certificate. There is no intervening editing/configuration step. Exclusive durable
reservation then permits one callback through the recorder.

The first gate action after recorder completion is independent state recapture.
It persists the post-state, requires capsule equality, rechecks workspace/live
checks/evidence and exactly-one accounting, and writes post-verification/final
result only on success. Detected material drift invalidates the result, records
HALT and prevents repair/retry. A failed callback retains indeterminate accounting;
it is not mislabeled zero observations.

Pre/post elapsed limits reject overruns. Capture/Git subprocesses have explicit
timeouts and the staged worker supervisor supplies a hard process envelope; the
in-process checkpoint timer is not itself a hostile callback interrupt mechanism.
Future dispatch must retain that external bounded supervisor.

R5.49's synthetic A → B → A counterexample remains valid historical evidence.
Endpoint checks cannot detect every transient accidental or deliberate mutation.
Known interference must invalidate a run even if bytes later match. Malicious ABA
resistance and proof of exclusive host ownership are outside this Tier-2 claim.

## Accounting, no repair and certificate design

R5.43 accounting distinguishes zero, one, indeterminate and multiple reservations/
observations. The gate requires zero before dispatch and exactly one authorized
completed observation afterward. A second callback is prevented; malformed or
multiple accounting cannot validate a successful result. HALT/final records are
terminal. Post-exposure repair is rejected by the protocol API, stale state cannot
validate, and restoration cannot reopen a stopped run. Cooperative no-repair rules
cover semantics, profiles, compiler, readiness, audit, admission, evaluator and
benchmark. Physical hostile-host writes are not claimed impossible.

The new certificate deterministically binds capsule identity, complete same-state/
same-experiment stage identities and mechanisms, R5.47 successor identity,
canonical protocol, recorder/authority identities, count 30, clean contamination
and all required lock identities. FAIL/INCOMPLETE, missing, stale, mixed or
cross-experiment receipts reject. Freshness is structural: capsule equality,
not timestamps or “recent enough.” Development authoring changes are irrelevant
under the established consumer boundary.

The assembly API is production-shaped and no longer demands unlimited host
closure. **It has not obtained production qualification.** R5.51's observation
entry accepts synthetic names only and has no B02 request loader/dispatch adapter.
A future production experiment still requires separate explicit authorization.
