## Recommended next experiment: independent specification reduction audit

Seek explicit authorization for a **specification-only R6.7 audit**, not implementation.
Freeze these R6.6 definitions and limits before review. An independently tasked reviewer
should receive only the synthetic formats, candidate definitions and kernel semantics,
with no package/curated sources or acceptance payloads. Independence must be disclosed;
none is claimed from this round's single-agent work. Reviewer assignment/delegation
requires that later authorization.

Bounded work:

1. Produce complete node-level plans and paired layout derivations for CFG66/DSV66/BXC66,
   including all error sites; clarify whether 64 structural nodes suffice when codec
   descriptors and finite selectors are accounted separately.
2. Attempt to eliminate ScanClass, prefix-validation context, layout assembly and
   Decimal15/UInt16BE by explicit alternative derivations. Count meanings rather than
   labels, table size or host library names. Preserve any counterexample to reduction.
3. Add one fixed-depth nested record format and one non-ASCII demand as adversarial
   specification requests. Determine refusal versus needed meaning without expanding
   the vocabulary merely to fit them.
4. Formalize determinism/termination/round-trip arguments and work-budget cost transitions.
   Identify any exact input with two legal outcomes as a specification failure.
5. Publish a reviewed accounting and scope verdict. Do not implement, compile solutions,
   run P6-A04 checks, access P6-A05 or change kernel/approval history.

Success criterion: an independently reviewable, unambiguous sufficient bounded
algebra with explicit reductions and unresolved lower bounds. Failure criterion:
unresolved conflicting outcomes, hidden operation/callback, impossible within-budget
plans or required unaccounted meaning. A later synthetic executable conformance
experiment could be proposed **only after** that audit and separate authorization;
it would need frozen independently specified expectations before authoring.
