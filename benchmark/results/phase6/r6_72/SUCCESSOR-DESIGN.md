# Proposed small successor — contrastive semantic selection

**Design only. No training dataset expansion or training in R6.72.** Obtain separate
owner authorization for data qualification, neutral decoder completion investigation
and then this experiment. The R6.72 halt is immutable; any later attempt is a fresh,
linked successor, not repair or completion of its record.

## Hypothesis and arms

One fresh q/v LoRA adapter on the same pinned
Qwen2.5-Coder-1.5B-Instruct revision
`2e1fd397ee46e1388853d2af2c993145b0f1098a`, compared with its unchanged base on a
fresh frozen matched diagnostic. Do not initialize from R6.67 or select checkpoints
from evaluation results. Test C-style contrastive supervision with explicit compact
semantic targets. A full A/B/C training ablation is outside this small experiment;
success would not isolate shortening from contrastive/data effects.

## Prospective data budget and admission

- 36 positive training requirements, 12 per operation, partitioned as six
  development families with six requirements each. Each family balances operations
  and includes matched same-scope relation changes, scope-order permutations,
  asymmetric le reversal, irrelevant refs, ref/const switches, exact Bool/Int64
  choices, and direct versus indirect dependency distinctions.
- Two single-factor negative decisions per requirement:72 negatives, giving108
  supervised records. Negatives cover wrong operation/identity/order/kind/type/deps;
  balance labels and mutation classes. Do not create these records in R6.72.
- A distinct reviewer derives source choices and explicit behavior fixtures before
  any model exposure. Disclose unattested independence. Require unchanged scorer
  acceptance for every positive. Structural/type/dependency invalid negatives must
  reject with native diagnostics; semantically wrong but executable negatives must
  have a source-derived distinguishing fixture or exact ordered/identity contract.
- Maintain exact provenance and licenses, hashes, review findings, data admission
  receipt, complete prompts/targets and complete discovery/review/generation costs.
  Feedback is deterministic validator evidence, never invented reasoning.

## Compact target and reconstruction

Proposed target shape (schema description, not a training example): operation enum;
two ordered tagged ref/const operands; result-type enum; explicit direct-dependency
choice. For contrastive records add valid/invalid decision and finite validator
feedback code. Accepted construction target remains unambiguously extractable.
No free-text reasoning is required. Fix its schema before dataset generation.

A versioned, qualified deterministic renderer inserts selected/value/native
envelopes. Preserve operand order, exact reference strings and scalar types; encode
literal tags explicitly. Serialize chosen dependencies in lexical order, rejecting
duplicates. Validate equality to inferred direct refs, never silently fill/remove
dependencies. Record raw set/order accuracy separately from canonical rendering.
Require round-trip equivalence and rejection controls using unchanged assembler,
then the authoritative scorer and functional execution. Execution remains LLM-free.

## Leakage-free evaluation design

18 fresh requirements, six per operation, across three evaluation-only structural
families (six each). Family assignment precedes concrete task authoring. Families
are different composition/scope/operand-role templates, not merely renamed domains.
All paraphrases, order permutations and positive/negative twins stay within one
partition. Do not use R6.63/R6.68/R6.71/R6.72 tasks or their mutated families as
evaluation examples. Hash/canonical and structural-template overlap audits must pass
before data admission. Independent evaluation custody is desirable; if unavailable,
declare coordinator-controlled, non-independent evidence.

Freeze requirements, compact choices, native actions and source-derived fixtures
before model calls. Evaluation fixtures include asymmetric le values, equality/non-
equality in both orientations, boundary add/overflow, and incompatible binding
negative decisions separately from ordinary construction tasks. Never inspect eval
results during data/training/checkpoint selection. No post-evaluation repair.

## Fixed training and inference settings

Predeclare inherited R6.66 precision/runtime and executable pins, NF4 double-quantized
base, BF16 computation/FP32 LoRA, SDPA, fresh rank8/alpha16/dropout0.05 q_proj/v_proj
LoRA, seed/data-seed651, unpadded microbatch1, accumulation4, AdamW adapter-only,
learning rate1e-4, 20 updates, linear warmup2 then decay to zero. Pin all remaining
optimizer values to the existing R6.66 configuration. Use completion+EOS-only causal
loss and deterministic cycling of a pre-frozen record order; disclose uneven record
exposure at this bounded80-microbatch budget. Select only final step20 for scoring.
No early stopping, hyperparameter search, optimizer changes or evaluation-driven
checkpoint selection. These settings are a proposal until a new signed freeze.

Both base and fresh adapter receive identical prompts, compact-output schema,
greedy one beam, max128 tokens, penalty1.1, cache off and seed651. The new compact
structural decoder/renderer must be neutrally qualified before task exposure; it
may enforce schema only, never task answers, scope membership or inferred gold.
Keep inherited120s/call, 3600s/worker, max8GiB reserved and min1.5GiB free limits.
Use corrected timing controls, tensor before/after checks, no retries/raw repair.

## Decision rule and reporting

Primary endpoint: paired full authoritative acceptance across18 construction cases.
Predeclare an exploratory positive signal only if adapter has at least3 net additional
full acceptances, at least1 gain in each operation, and no more than1 base-pass
regression. Report every pair and operation/ordered operand/type/direct-dependency
dimension; do not promote structural completion to semantic improvement. Report
negative-decision accuracy independently; it cannot substitute for construction.
Small deterministic evidence supports a follow-up proposal, not population inference.

Record tokens, supervised exposures, actual training time/VRAM, curation/validation
costs, raw generations, timing setup/enforcement/forward costs and native first
failure. Halt on integrity or runtime qualification failure. Preserve all outputs
and no model-weight update outside the new adapter. Production kernel26 and all
execution meanings remain unchanged. Stop at publication pending further authority.
