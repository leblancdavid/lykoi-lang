## Evidence taxonomy for the revised charter

- **Demonstrated:** bounded validators, explicit composition witnesses, deterministic
  observed executions, source/identity tooling, finite acceptance results above.
- **Experimental prototypes:** FRC review/formalizer machinery, R6.10 VM and the
  research adapters in comparisons; qualification is specific to their contracts.
- **Unresolved hypotheses:** adaptive vocabulary transfer, AI generation ease,
  total-cost advantage, broad software expressiveness and complete impact analysis.
- **Known limitations:** static/runtime distinction, source-authority ambiguity,
  partial integration, finite bounds/overhead, backend leaks, correlated expectations,
  setup-cost gaps and contaminated contexts. Their historical records stay intact.

## Historical conclusions no longer architectural defaults

1. Minimize kernel count: retain historical counting and all 26 production constructs;
   choose new representation by falsifiable utility, not presumed irreducibility.
2. Always require layered FRC/controller/production traversal: retained for its
   original product/authority scope, optional as a future research architecture choice.
   Source fidelity and independent acceptance still matter in the new experiment.
3. Prefer lean B solely from R6.16: retain that scoped recommendation; it does not
   settle adaptive vocabulary, broader semantics or removal of Lykoi.
4. Infer lack of meaning from absent opcodes: R6.14 supports three finite reductions;
   this does not generalize to every gap or erase failed representations.
5. Treat separate contexts or passing tests as independence: R6.7–R6.9 and later
   contamination disclosures require actual input/process boundaries for attribution.
