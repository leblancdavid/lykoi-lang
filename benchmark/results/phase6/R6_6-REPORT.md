## Candidate additions and composition evidence

| Candidate | Missing meaning made explicit | Existing reuse / exclusions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I-BOUND | Checked raw sequence recognition/access, bounded consuming traversal, disjoint predictive choice, typed result/prefix/span construction and ordered assembly under explicit plans | K01–K10/K12/K18/K19 compose decoded records, finite transformations, predicates, cardinality and presence. No host callbacks, unrestricted fold/recursion, ambiguous grammar resolution or effects. |
| C-ATOM | Explicit ASCII/finite-escape, Decimal15 and unsigned byte/UInt16BE interpretation and canonical encoding relations | Finite literal-to-boolean projection derives without a Boolean primitive. K24 handles postdecode addition; it does not already define variable-length decimal scanning. |

The two labels are **semantic families, not an irreducible construct count**.
Sequence slicing/traversal/concatenation and scalar arithmetic relations cannot be
hidden inside a contract/map/codec registry. Byte/result types are representation
refinements with material construction/binding obligations. No proposed kernel
total is published. Current implemented/admitted accounting stays exactly **26**.

Tokenization is derived within I-BOUND from class scans, choices and spans; a separate
tokenizer is not needed for the witnesses. Parsing and assembly share a bounded
structural algebra but require explicit paired layouts, not assumed grammar inversion.
Artifacts transform through selection, typed projection/replacement, checked addition,
cardinality and reassembly. Existing ordered validation provides decoded rejection;
parse-specific cursor/limits/error-site meaning belongs to interpretation.

CFG66 demonstrates optional absent versus empty values, escaping, named fields and
duplicate rejection. DSV66 demonstrates doubled quotes, comma-containing fields,
multiple records, numeric conversion/provenance and stable filtering/output order.
BXC66 demonstrates dependent bounded byte lengths, version/header, raw content,
redundant length/total checks, exact rejection and reassembly. BXC66 is deliberately
not ZIP/wheel and does not claim cryptographic integrity or executable installation.

Composition with K17/K20/K21/K22 is qualified: parsed values can feed declared
contracts/authority/one-store effects once new bindings are implemented and verified.
Bytes confer no authority; parsing a name does not authorize a path, and single-store
atomicity does not become a filesystem transaction. Current normal-path support for
these proposed forms is absent.

## Adversarial findings and alternatives

All nine requested challenge topics have determinate outcomes for each bounded
format. Empty text inputs are accepted; empty containers reject. ASCII high bytes
reject before syntax, but arbitrary binary content remains valid. Truncation rejects
at actual EOF. Duplicate keys are never silently overwritten. Dynamic invalid lengths
reject before allocation. Budgets and validation ordering are explicit.

Ambiguous selectors and recursive plans are refused, not silently assigned a parser
policy. Fixed-depth nesting can be described by acyclic plans; recursive grammar/AST
support is not derived. Strong integrity, general Unicode and streaming are incomplete
for broader demands. Optimization preservation of logical work errors and current
kernel/profile result integration remain unresolved. These limitations bound the
supported candidate; they do not turn finite specification examples into proof.

Dedicated constructs (A) offer precise APIs but can duplicate lexing/codec/layout
meaning. General interpretation (B) could derive more from uniform operations, but
needs explicit arithmetic, traversal, typing and termination semantics; determinism
alone is not a semantic model. Hybrid (C) is the recommended bounded candidate:
shared structural rules and visible typed leaves. C may be equivalent to a factored A,
and B may eventually reduce its primitive count. No such minimum is established here.
AI-native machine-checkability matters; human source/generated readability does not.
No runtime AI-provider dependency is introduced.
