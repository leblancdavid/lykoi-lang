## Classification rule

Protocol integrity failure would be PROTOCOL_HALT. Incomplete core data specification
would be DATA_DESIGN_PARTIAL. Missing human/custody qualification is the present
INDEPENDENT_EVALUATION_GAP, even with detailed protocols. If evaluation and data were
qualified but checkpoint/stack/rights blocked the next test, TRAINING_PREPARATION_GAP
would apply. DATA_AND_EVALUATION_DESIGN_READY would describe sufficiently reviewed
design, not permission or proof of execution readiness. No gate is inferred from another.

Production-backed curriculum gaps: relations, authority, explicit historical migration,
dimensioned time, multi-record ordering/effects and cross-feature pairs. Experimental
composition coverage cannot fill these production gaps by relabeling. Ten correlated
candidates are not a justified generalization corpus.
