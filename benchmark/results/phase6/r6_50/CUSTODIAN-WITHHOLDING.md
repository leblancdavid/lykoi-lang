## Structural sibling design brief

Within the commissioner's chosen stateful domain, request a second episode that
changes at least two interacting behavioral axes: for example transition branching
and rollback obligations, or cross-record dependency and rejection precedence.
Require a different observable history and at least one counterexample to reusing
the primary episode's transition table unchanged. Persistence/restart should be
tested through a changed history, not just new field names or numeric thresholds.
These axes are AI-authored suggestions, not mandated source behavior. The human
commissioner must independently write the actual sibling; the reviewer determines
whether transfer is meaningful. If the sibling copies a training scaffold, reject
the independent-transfer claim even when acceptance passes.

## Custody boundary requirements

- Use a separate custodian-controlled host/account/storage inaccessible to training
  and authoring identities, including their tools, caches, logs, retrieval indexes,
  inherited guidance and backup mounts. Different directories in this repo fail.
- Store source/FRC, tests/oracle, reference solution, seeds, clarification details
  and review records there. Do not place hidden details in public prompts or tool
  outputs. No credential is published or passed to the producer.
- Publish only opaque IDs, role attestations, a release policy and a salted bundle
  commitment. Keep the random salt private until audit opening to avoid guessing
  low-entropy requirements. Retain individual SHA256 identities privately.
- Before release, test unauthorized read/list/search/API/retrieval/export attempts
  using harmless canary assets under participant-equivalent identities. Record
  expected denial, actual output, configuration identities and access audit logs.
  Test permitted scoring as well. Canaries qualify controls, not model cleanliness.
- Audit provider/session context and permitted input manifests. A sandbox that
  denies local files alone does not establish exclusion of hidden provider context.
  Unattested context remains a disclosed evaluation qualification blocker.
- Freeze trained artifacts, all training exports and prompts before releasing
  sibling requirement/FRC to evaluator-only authoring sessions. Never release the
  hidden implementation or acceptance details to those sessions. Scoring executes
  custodian-side on submitted candidates with bounded feedback fixed in advance.
- At study end, an authorized auditor checks commitments, release/access timestamps
  and training manifests. Public opening is optional; audit can remain private.

## Failures and revocation

family, including predecessor/successor, templates, definitions and trajectories.
Preserve the task as **UNQUALIFIED_EVALUATION_MATERIAL**; do not relabel it clean.
Replace it with genuinely new custodian-authored material under later authorization.
Instruction-only secrecy, AI promises and fresh sessions are insufficient.
