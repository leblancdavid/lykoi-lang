# R6.48 leakage and partition audit

`PARTITION-PLAN.json` was sealed by `SOURCE-FREEZE.json` before construction:
F1–F4 are development; F5–F6 are evaluation quarantine. The two episodes of a
family stay together. All source, FRC, acceptance, definition, expansion, script
and trace derivatives inherit the family assignment. No evaluation episode has
a symbolic solution or construction trace, and no evaluation material is in the
training-ready export. The export is empty because zero episodes are admitted.

This is **PROCESS_DEMONSTRATION_ONLY**, not qualified held-out generalization.
The coordinator has seen every family and publishes its sources. There is no
independent custodian, access-control attestation or secret evaluation corpus.
An evaluation label does not restore unseen status. Future evaluations must use
fresh owner/custodian-controlled requirements rather than treating F5/F6 as held out.

Eight distinct operation-sequence shapes and four broad executed behavioral
patterns are useful process diagnostics, not eight independent semantic families.
Shared scaffold overlap is substantial even though no source is a simple renamed
copy. No cross-family reusable definition is imported; all application dependencies
are explicitly pinned within F4 packages. Public foundation code/semantics are shared
across all experimental episodes. R6.32 registry persistence is not application state;
these packages use exact in-package dependency identities, not mutable latest lookup.

This audit verifies derivative accounting and export exclusion. It cannot certify
absence of latent semantic overlap with the exposed historical corpus, inherited
model knowledge or future evaluation requirements. For scaling, family labels must
be backed by an independently reviewed connected-component analysis including
skeletons, abstraction ancestry, successors, solutions and trajectories.
