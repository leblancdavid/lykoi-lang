# Commissioning, authority and acceptance review

Six family records are in `commissioning/F1.json` through `F6.json`. Twelve
original sources are in `episodes/E01/SOURCE.json` through `E12/SOURCE.json`.
These are **new synthetic human-style requests**, authored by the coordinator.
They are not externally commissioned or human-authored requirements.

No independent commissioner, requirement owner, acceptance reviewer or secret
evaluation custodian was supplied. A fresh agent context alone would not attest
exclusion of inherited guidance or repository access. This pilot uses the explicitly
allowed same-coordinator fallback, with zero independence claims. Commissioning
was informed by the requested coverage and knowledge of Lykoi's bounds; it was
not capability-blind. No family was copied from an existing example, but that
does not establish impartial selection or independent behavioral diversity.

Each complete source explicitly specifies its input representation, output,
checks and order. Those choices are part of the commissioned source, not unstated
formalizer additions. The eight executable FRCs preserve that source order.
E09/E10 retain unresolved expiry, actor authentication, idempotency, eligibility,
fairness, transaction and error decisions. Their FRC envelopes are pending,
not complete contracts. Their acceptance files say NOT_FROZEN, contain no invented
answers, and do not authorize construction. No clarification reply was obtained.

E11/E12 have frozen complete obligation-level contracts, but no executable
distributed/physical oracle. Thus **10 complete FRCs does not mean 10 executable
acceptance suites**. Their secondary acceptance gap is retained with their primary
capability disposition. The offline/exactly-once conditions may themselves be
unachievable without coordination; this is a commissioning limitation, not a
demonstrated expressive impossibility of the abstract Lykoi kernel.

## Acceptance justification and limitations

The 81 literal finite expectations were derived before candidate construction
from the new explicit sources and preserved deterministic wrapper/VM semantics.
They cover 24 successes and 57 rejections per pass, including zero/ceiling/exact
equality, truncation, trailing input, mixed-invalid precedence and zero work budget.
Two episodes explicitly retain Bool locals. Root scalar provenance must have
the complete consumed span and empty origins; error offset/stage/path/expected
sets and the designated symbolic role are frozen. Content-derived VM node names
are resolved from artifact identity and the frozen role, never from candidate output.

Expected results are literal records, not a Python reimplementation supplied as
a solution. Source/acceptance hashes are sealed before construction; candidates
and expansions are sealed before their first acceptance execution. All seals are
verified again on replay and publication. Candidate results did not change any
expectation. No defective expectation or failed first candidate was observed.

Review is **candidate-output-independent derivation by the same coordinator**,
not independent review. Exact nonzero logical work and output-span envelopes
are replay-equality observations; they lack an independent absolute cost oracle.
Finite acceptance does not prove universal correctness, complete Int64 overflow
coverage or all resource cutoffs. The generated source requirements have no
external human behavioral approval. These limits block TRAINING_ADMITTED even
though the eight observed finite suites pass.

## Cost evidence

Twelve sources, ten complete contracts, eight executable acceptance suites,
eight constructions and three fresh-process replay records are retained.
Source/candidate freezes and replay records retain real UTC stage timestamps.
Individual commissioning, formalization and review duration/tokens were not
metered. Independent review cost is unmeasured because no independent review
occurred. No synthetic cost estimates are presented as observations.
