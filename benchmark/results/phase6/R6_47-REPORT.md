# R6.47 — Lykoi model training readiness

**Final classification: `R6_47_DATASET_PARTIAL`.**

Existing research contains usable ingredients for learning the narrow experimental
symbolic format, but does not establish sufficiently diverse, independently qualified
data and clean evaluation separation for training readiness. The audited positive
cohort contains **17 unique requirement episodes**, backed by49 immutable definitions.
All17 retain provenance limitations; **0 meet the stronger independent/leakage-qualified
admission standard**. These are source candidates, not a released SFT dataset or a
complete semantic-equivalence census of every historical fixture.

The verified RTX4070/nominal32GiB machine is plausibly sufficient for a small1.5–4B
parameter-efficient experiment after separately authorized setup. The active environment
has no discoverable training stack; installed GGUF inference models do not establish
trainable checkpoint availability. Data readiness is the main unresolved research gate.

## 6. Readiness and smallest next step

Broad training readiness fails on diversity, production coverage and clean family
separation. Identity/replay foundations are strong but independence and serialization
admission are partial. Hardware plausibility is not dataset readiness. `DATASET_PARTIAL`
is justified by actual finite-qualified sources; neither a training-ready nor a
hardware-only terminal classification describes the main finding.

The smallest justified next authorization is a **data-only12-episode process pilot**:
six independently commissioned requirement/scaffold families, two contracts each,
four development families and two custodian-held evaluation families, paired direct/
actual-tool views, source-authoritative acceptance and AI-free replay. This is not a
universal minimum for useful SFT. Its purpose is to establish the data pipeline and
discover whether adequate diversity/corrections can be obtained without semantics
tailoring. Only after that gate should setup and a small PEFT experiment be considered.
