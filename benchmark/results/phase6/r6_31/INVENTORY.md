## Exact foundations

[BASELINE.json](BASELINE.json) records raw SHA256 identities for kernel accounting,
VM, wrapper, adapter, semantic dispatcher, provider transport and schema-publication
adapter. Its anchors bind the full production and R6.3–R6.30 preservation inventory.
The current ledger has26 constructs; a count is not a minimality or completeness proof.

- **H1:** R6.18 exact Int64/Bool/Unit, ordered seq, UInt8 atom, value/check/end,
  fixed UInt16BE emit, ref/const/add/le/eq, literal/ref call parameters. R6.23
  construct-1 assembles these meanings. No records, persistent state, general
  branching, dynamic loops or opaque host callbacks are added.
- **H2:** existing production scalar/mutable/predicate stateful profiles and the
  R6.16 research B/C interface. Guarded enum writes, conditional rule exceptions,
  persistent reload and runtime invariants are supported locally. The VM wrapper
  is not silently used as a stateful foundation.
- Production inspect/diff/impact and pinned change mechanisms exist. Legacy impact
  omits extension predicate/mutation facts; this deficiency must be measured or
  explicitly labeled unsupported, not assumed solved by IDs.

The design is implementable in small experiment-local facades over existing APIs.
The prerequisites are not already commissioned or qualified. A neutral failure to
express a required stateful rule, dependency update or equivalent Track B behavior
halts that successor's readiness; it cannot be covered by Python task logic inside
a symbolic adapter. Broader records/recursion/effects, new language constructs,
general equivalence proofs and training are future research outside this roadmap.
