# R6.40 preserved protocol limitations and deviations

This record is post-score disclosure. It changes no frozen input, candidate, score
or vocabulary and authorizes no further experiment.

1. **Authorized comparator substitution:** B is a coordinator-AI-designed proxy,
   explicitly approved before discovery, not the requested human-designed library.
   The pool froze before participant discovery; no AI-discovered body or evaluation
   result informed it. The coordinator nevertheless knew the planned task skeletons.
   PROTOCOL.md's pre-selection sequencing must not be read as cognitive blindness;
   evaluation files were serialized later, but planning preceded the pool freeze.
2. **Aggregate capacity and documentation mismatch:** the predetermined per-entry
   rule accepts one-step differences. C has3 entries/10 body steps; B has3/7. All
   signatures, result types and nesting match. The aggregate3-step difference exceeds
   R6.31's one-step/10% tolerance; B/C applicability documents occupy681/2,379 canonical
   bytes. Exact provider-document tokens are unavailable. `capacity_matched:true` in
   B-VOCABULARY.json means the implemented per-entry test only, not full aggregate
   or prompt matching. No post-outcome padding or semantic adjustment was made.
3. **Source-offset contract/oracle ambiguity:** the author contract says "site u/v/t"
   but does not explicitly require original byte-source offsets through compose.
   The frozen oracle assumes them. The unchanged VM gives a completed seq-result
   its region span; zero-byte computations after two reads therefore start at2.
   B/C's combination artifacts preserve expected values/error codes but fail the
   origin-offset expectations. Frozen scores remain failures. The gap cannot be
   confidently attributed to incorrect author semantics rather than underexplicit
   task semantics/oracle expectations.
4. **Expanded-template fidelity:** flattening to wrapper primitive steps removes
   compose-generated seq regions and their result spans. It is value/check-order
   equivalent in the development constant hosts, not full-envelope equivalent in
   byte-reading callers. X's6/6 cannot establish a pure compression/reference
   ablation. No new execution primitive was introduced; different existing
   constructions expose different inherited provenance behavior.
5. **Budget overshoot and native stop:** E4-B crossed the reported48,000 processed
   input+output cap at50,643. The supervisor terminated the child tree at the first
   observed crossing, after a candidate had sealed. Preserve its failed artifact,
   expenditure and CLOSED.json. `provider_halt:true` there represents a nonzero
   stopped process, not an observed provider error. No retry occurred. Its scores
   are descriptive sealed-artifact scores, not a successful within-budget session.
6. **First-attempt distinction:** acceptance first/final submitted scores are retained.
   The supplementary first-construction audit also scores the first complete Entry
   admission attempt, including E3-B's rejected malformed identity. C/E4 contains
   one self-authored additional submission. No offline-oracle feedback was supplied.
7. **Accounting limits:** exact upstream calls/billing, effective authentication/
   reasoning, coordinator model tokens and complete setup/freeze/export effort are
   unavailable. Cache usage is counted. The aggregate21-reuse arithmetic versus B
   in AMORTIZATION.json is not an established break-even: it includes failed tasks
   and divides aggregate differences by successful reuses. The matched-success
   refinement shows negative aggregate token savings for direct transfer against
   every control; no measured economic break-even is established.

These limitations justify `R6_40_COMPARISON_INCONCLUSIVE`, despite successful
composition admission and two bounded direct-transfer witnesses. They do not
erase the primitive/template score advantage or establish adaptive benefit.
