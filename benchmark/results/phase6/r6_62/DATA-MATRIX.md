## Remaining gaps and prospective generation

Need source-bound before/after states; qualified bounded updater/assembler; actual
tool schema/rendering and response captures; independent evaluation commissioning
and custody; structural diversity beyond the two scaffolds; production IR prompts;
successor, relation, authority, migration, dimensioned-time and multi-record coverage.
Hidden provider context/isolation and actual billing remain unattested historically.

A separately authorized deterministic expert decomposition could produce B and
supported C views from endpoints: declare inputs, append ordered nodes, finalize,
validate and replay original frozen behavior. All construction choices must be
labeled expert/scripted, with new artifact identities and rejection controls. It
does not recover what an AI thought or selected. Producing57 fragments would not
create57 independent tasks, and R6.25 tools must not be presumed to cover byte
atoms or stateful transitions. A matched three-view cohort may need new independently
specified narrow requirements, rather than converting unsupported historical ones.
Genuine AI trajectories require future observed selections, not invented logs.
