# R6.48 — Lykoi training-data generation and qualification pilot

**Final classification: `R6_48_DATA_PIPELINE_PARTIAL`.**

The bounded pilot commissioned **12 synthetic human-style episodes in six
families**, produced **10 complete frozen FRCs**, and constructed **8 experimental
symbolic candidates**. All eight pass their frozen finite suites on three
fresh-process AI-free runs: **81 cases per run, 243 observations total**, with
identical complete envelopes including errors, provenance and logical work.
**Zero episodes are TRAINING_ADMITTED.** Independent commissioning, acceptance
review and clean custodian-held evaluation separation were unavailable.

The mechanical construction/serialization/replay pipeline works in this narrow
scope. It has not qualified trustworthy admitted-data production or scaling.
Two sources require clarification; two complete obligation-level requirements
need unsupported distributed effects/authority and have no executable oracle.
The eight successful episodes still share a narrow stateless execution scaffold.

## 4. Functional verification and process controls

[Replay 1](r6_48/REPLAY-1.json), [replay 2](r6_48/REPLAY-2.json) and
[replay 3](r6_48/REPLAY-3.json) are three actual fresh Python processes using the
unchanged wrapper and VM, without AI. Each reloads strict serialized symbols,
validates, re-expands, verifies the stored executable and executes the frozen cases.
Each run has **24 success cases and 57 rejection cases**. Repeated runs add
observations, not training examples. Their common observation SHA256 is
`0b399364dd9f52d73c49ba56ee5a5700f2998046a9a4695c14d67c6bbcaf8818`.

Cases cover positive behavior, zero/ceiling/equality boundaries, invalid flags,
truncation, trailing bytes, mixed-invalid error order, root/error provenance and
zero logical-work cutoff. Nonzero work is checked by full-envelope replay
equality, not independently predicted absolute costs. No mutable state exists in
the executable cohort, so persistence/restart/state-effect observations are
**NOT_REACHED**, not passing. Finite test success is not universal correctness.

[Ten process controls](r6_48/PROCESS-CONTROLS.json) pass: wrong identity/type/deps
reject through the unchanged validator; incorrect success value/error offset and
logical-work divergence are detected; incomplete dataset records reject; source,
acceptance and partition seals remain intact; an admission label cannot waive
provenance; evaluation records cannot enter the training export. Mutants are
negative controls, not failed original candidates or natural AI corrections.

No acceptance expectation was altered after seeing output. Expected error roles
are resolved through content-derived artifact sites, without consulting outputs.
Same-coordinator expectation design and absent independent acceptance/cost review
remain admission blockers even though the literal suites pass.

## 7. Training-readiness implications and next step

The pilot establishes narrow mechanics, not a scalable admitted-data process.
The dominant bottlenecks are independent source/acceptance authority and genuine
family isolation. Ambiguity resolution blocks realistic stateful sources. The
successful cohort lacks production state/effect coverage and real AI semantic
tool-selection/correction trajectories. Whole-package scripted traces duplicate
content; raw evidence is about **1.06 MB** before summary/publication files.
Individual formalization/review durations and tokens were not metered; independent
review costs and training resource usage were not measured.

R6.47's preserved RTX4070/nominal32GiB hardware assessment remains a planning
input, not a new measured training result. This CPU/stdlib pilot required no
GPU inference or training stack. Storage is currently small, but raw traces,
multiple replay passes and future state snapshots can dominate direct-artifact
size. Neither twelve episodes nor zero admitted episodes justify model training.

**Ready to scale: no.** Smallest recommended separately authorized next step:
one independently human-commissioned, fully clarified **local-stateful** family
with two episodes; a separate source-authoritative acceptance reviewer; exact
pre-author freezes; actual semantic-tool trajectory capture; deterministic
persistence/error/restart checks. Use existing production semantics, and meter
formalization/review effort. This addresses the admission gate and production
coverage before expanding example count. A later held-out family must be genuinely
custodian-controlled and fresh; this pilot's exposed F5/F6 do not qualify.
