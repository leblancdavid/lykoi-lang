# R6.47 — Read-only training-readiness assessment

Owner authorization: bounded documentation and read-only data analysis of existing
Lykoi research for direct symbolic generation and typed-tool construction. This
protocol records the scope of this assessment, not a pre-exposure experiment freeze.
The coordinator receives historical findings through repository guidance and reads
outcomes for inventory. No independent replication or blind data selection is claimed.

Permitted actions: inspect preserved requirements, representations, diagnostics,
transcripts, acceptance and replay records; compute identities, counts and coverage;
query hardware and installed-package/model metadata; read public model documentation;
publish additive documents and analysis receipts. Analysis never imports or invokes
experimental execution, generation, scoring, training or provider dispatch.

Prohibited actions: production/experimental implementation edits, training, model
downloads, dependency installation, historical rescoring, P6-A04 acceptance and
P6-A05 content access. All history and kernel accounting remain unchanged.

Counting units:

1. Artifact identity: exact immutable definition ID, not a behavioral example.
2. Positive episode: one requirement and its complete accepted construction;
   a lifecycle includes its predecessor, successor, callers and migration together.
3. Alternative authoring conditions, renamed programs, reformatting, snapshots,
   expansions, generated code and repeated executions do not add positive episodes.
4. Different formulas/check order within a shared scaffold are distinct contracts,
   but not independently sourced families. Constants alone do not add family diversity.
5. Failures and correction turns retain separate labels; they are not positive targets.

The audited positive cohort is R6.36–R6.40: eight lifecycle contracts, three discovery
contracts and six evaluation contracts. One accepted representative condition per
contract is selected. Wider historical sources are inventoried as auxiliary pools,
not silently promoted to complete joined examples. Counts are scoped, not an
exhaustive semantic-equivalence census of every historical test fixture.

No trainable JSONL or synthetic examples are generated. The 17 positive candidates
are available source bundles for a restricted experimental-profile extraction pilot,
not a released or independently certified training dataset. Stop after publication.
