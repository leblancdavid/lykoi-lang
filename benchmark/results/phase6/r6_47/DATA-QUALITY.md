Use two axes: **technical qualification** and **provenance/contamination**. A
replayable correct-on-suite program can still have weak task independence. Do not
make the six labels an exclusive partition by silently discarding provenance limits.

## Preserve these specific limitations

- L36: callable tool discovery, zero participant semantic MCP calls; JSON broker
  authoring, exposed capability-tailored task, limited separate full-envelope oracle.
- L37/L38/L39: exact model argument-to-definition checks and AI-free execution;
  coordinator-created requirements/oracles, same platform, hidden-context exclusion
  unattested. L38 comparator layouts can differ; L39 clones share original baselines.
- R6.40: source-span contract/oracle ambiguity, AI-designed B proxy, unmatched
  aggregate capacity/documents, flat versus compact envelope nonequivalence.
  Replay includes failures and does not turn them into accepted solutions.
- Production witnesses: effects/authority declarations and host actor bindings do
  not supply authentication. Cooperating local persistence is not crash/concurrency
  or distributed durability. No new production capability follows from sample counts.

## Admission record required before actual SFT

For each episode: source authority and permission, requirement/schema/foundation
hashes, exact target and closure, validation receipt, frozen acceptance expectations,
raw functional verdicts, AI-free replay identity, family/lineage IDs, visible-model
configuration, author/curator/reviewer disclosures, diagnostics and correction edges.
Missing fields remain null with reasons. An ambiguous contract or observed defect
blocks a full-contract positive label; narrower admission must explicitly state its
projection and excluded obligations. No inferred chain of thought or missing tool
turn may fill a gap. All extraction/repair labels are prospective; history is immutable.
